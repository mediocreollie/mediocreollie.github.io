OCR runs locally using Tesseract.js. The first scan downloads the free recognition engine and English language files. It may take a minute. Check every suggestion; it may read a previous price or unit price.

Desktop browser capture helper

Drag Capture Cocobella price to your bookmarks bar. On the retailer product page, highlight the displayed price, then click that bookmark. It opens this confirmation form with price suggestions. It only reads your selection and the product-page address, never cookies or your account.

If your browser blocks bookmarklets, use a screenshot or paste the price text below.
