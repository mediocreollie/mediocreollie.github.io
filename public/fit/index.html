How to measure

A simple, consistent measurement is more useful than trying to make it look smaller.

Chest or bust

Around the fullest part, with the tape level across your back.

Waist

Around your natural waist, usually above the belly button.

Hips

Around the fullest part of your seat, with feet together.

Measurement guide

T-shirt measurement map

Match the chart meaning first. Body measurements go around you; garment measurements are taken from the clothing.

  • Chest: around the body, or across a flat garment then doubled.
  • Shoulder: point to point across the upper back or garment seam.
  • Length: from the high shoulder point down to the hem.
  • Sleeve: from the shoulder point to the sleeve end.

Diagrams explain measurement direction only. They do not predict fabric stretch, drape or appearance on a person.

A useful distinction

Body measurements in a size guide describe the person a size is intended for. Garment measurements describe the clothing itself and include wearing ease. When possible, compare like with like.
